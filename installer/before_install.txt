Ax-Easy Lyricist Sync 1.1.0

Auto-syncs pasted lyrics to your songs and exports TTML, LRC, SRT and VTT.

FIRST RUN
This installer is small. The first time you start the app it downloads the sync
engine into %LOCALAPPDATA%\Ax-Easy\LyricistSync:
  - NVIDIA GPU (CUDA 12.4 build): about 4.5 GB
  - CPU only: about 2.2 GB
Downloads are SHA256-verified and resume where they stopped.

WINDOWS SMARTSCREEN
The installer is not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may show
"Windows protected your PC". Click "More info" and then "Run anyway".
